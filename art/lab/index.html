Hidden lab

Prototypes for the look of the game. Nothing here ships — it is the folder you open when you want to try a colour, a curve, or an icon before it touches web/src. Assets under /game/ are the real ones from web/src/assets/, served live, so a prototype never drifts from what the game actually looks like.

  • colour Cell palette Ten candidates each for rock, paper, scissors and the desecrated tile, drawn as real board cells on the real background. Click a swatch to load it into a live board. Includes texture treatments and a colourblind simulation.
  • motion Cell fill & drain Four ways ink can arrive on a cell and four ways it can leave, all running off the same two durations so they can be compared honestly. Sliders for both.
  • motion Reveal snapshot Three ways to show the opponent's board as a timed snapshot that covers your own board, instead of a panel parked beside it. Each puts the countdown on the frame itself rather than in a bar or a number. Shared duration slider.
  • chrome Navbar icons Icon treatments for the top chrome at their real size, against the real bar.
Add a prototype by dropping a folder with an index.html next to these and linking it here. See README.md.
