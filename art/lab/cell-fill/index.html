Hidden — cell fill / drain prototype

Click any cell to fill it, click again to drain it. Every lane runs off the same two durations so they can be compared honestly. Empty face is #f5f5f5; the colour is a separate layer on top, which is the change that makes both animations possible at all.

Fill — click an empty cell

Drain — click a full cell

Shipped: fill-box at 250ms + drain-box at 500ms. Both are pure transform, so they run on the compositor and never touch layout. The drain reverses the fill's easing curve as well as its geometry, which is what keeps half a second from reading as lag: it holds the square, then takes it.
