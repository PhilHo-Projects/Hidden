Reveal snapshot

Three ways to show the opponent's board as a timed snapshot instead of a panel that sits beside your own board until you place. Each one covers the stage outright — that is the point, not a side effect: the shipping peek floats to the right of the player board and has nowhere to go on a phone.

All three put the countdown on the frame rather than in a progress bar or a number, and all three read the same --p value from one driver, so what you are comparing is the treatment and nothing else. Stages are a literal 390px wide.

Space or click a stage's REVEAL button to fire just that one.
Nothing here ships. When one of these wins, the motion moves into web/src/animations/ and the overlay into web/src/components/; the 1.5s expiry itself is a rule change in packages/game-core, not CSS.
